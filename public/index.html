✨ 4 Stress-Free Rules So Gabriel Loves Speaking English

1. The "Warm Recast" Rule (Never Say "Speak English!")

If Gabriel says "Mama mau minum susu", smile warmly and model it naturally: "Oh, 'Can I have milk, please?' Yes! Let's pour your milk!" He hears the exact phrase right when he cares about it, with zero pressure.

2. Give 2-Choice English Prompts When Stuck

Open questions like "How was class?" are hard at age 6. Instead ask: "Did you play flashcards or sing a song at Tebet today?" The words he needs are right inside your question!

3. Let the Tablet Alarm Be the Fun Signal

Whenever Gabriel's alarm shows the green 🗣️ SPEAK ENGLISH! pill, make it a team game: "Ooh, the alarm switched our English brains ON! Let's tap the speaker button together!"

4. Sore-Leg & Big-Feelings Safety Valve

If his recovering leg hurts or he feels tired/upset, comfort him first in whichever language comes out, then gently give the simple English words ("My leg feels tired, let's rest on the pillow").

🍎 Option A: iPad (Guided Access - 100% Hardware Locked)
  1. Open Safari on Gabriel's iPad to the URL above, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and open Gabriel Alarm from the Home Screen (removes Safari address bar!).
  2. On iPad, open Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn ON & set a Passcode.
  3. Open Gabriel Alarm, then Triple-Click the Top / Power Button → tap Start.
  4. Done! Home swipe, notifications, Dock, and switching apps are 100% disabled by iPadOS. Gabriel only sees the illustrated activity alarm!
🤖 Option B: Android / Samsung Tablet (App Pinning)
  1. Open Chrome on the tablet to the URL above → tap ⋮ Menu → Add to Home screen / Install App.
  2. Go to Settings → Security & Privacy → More Security Settings → App Pinning (Pin Windows) → turn ON + enable "Ask for PIN before unpinning".
  3. Open Gabriel Alarm, swipe up to Recent Apps, tap the app icon at top → tap Pin this app.
  4. Done! Back, Home, and Recent buttons are completely locked until you enter your tablet PIN!
